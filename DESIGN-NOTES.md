# Design notes

**Hero direction.** The subject is Pratyush Sawant, a computer science student who ships real software: a network dashboard at Ericsson, an oilfield valuation platform, a mobile CRM. The audience is recruiters and engineers skimming for Summer 2027 internships, so the hero has one job: say who he is and what he builds within a second, then get out of the way of the 3D character. The tone is quiet and exact, like a well-labelled instrument panel, not a showreel. The memorable detail is scale: the name is set enormous and tightly spaced in Geist at weight 600, with the surname in the teal accent. Everything else (the greeting, the role line) stays small and muted, so the name is the only loud thing on the screen. Colors and font are unchanged: `#0a0e17` background, `#5eead4` accent, `#eae5ec` text, Geist. Casing is title case instead of the old tracked ALL CAPS, which reads less like a template and fits more letters in the side columns.
